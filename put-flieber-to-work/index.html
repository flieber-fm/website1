Put Flieber to work on your operation

Ask a question, test a decision or hand off a recurring job, in plain language, in the Flieber app, in Slack or in Claude, ChatGPT or any MCP-compatible agent. Below are ideas to start from: examples of jobs operators give Flieber, what Flieber does with each one and what comes back. They’re not a complete list.

Daily risks and reviews

P1

Morning risk review

“Every morning, review my inventory position and tell me the biggest risks I need to act on. Prioritize by stockout risk, sales velocity, product tier, inbound shipments and revenue impact, and explain why each one matters.”

What Flieber does
Projects stock for every product and location, ranks the risks by the criteria you named and explains each one.
What you get
A short list of products to act on, with the reason and a recommended next step, every morning in the app or Slack.

Inventory forecastingWorkflowsRuns on scheduleRead only

P2

Weekly operations memo

“Every Monday, prepare an inventory operations memo. Combine Flieber data with supplier emails, freight updates and major forecast exceptions. Tell me the top risks, what changed and what decisions need to be made.”

What Flieber does
Gathers the week’s changes in stock, forecasts and inbound shipments, reads connected supplier emails and ranks what needs a decision.
What you get
One memo every Monday with the risks, the changes and the decisions waiting for you.

All modulesRuns on scheduleRead onlyNeeds Gmail or Outlook

P3

Monthly inventory health

“Every month, review our overall inventory health and explain what changed. Tell me whether we are carrying too much or too little inventory, where the biggest working capital issues are and what we should prioritize.”

What Flieber does
Compares inventory value, coverage, overstock and stockouts with the previous month and finds the products driving the change.
What you get
A monthly summary of inventory health with the working capital at stake and the actions to prioritize.

Inventory forecastingData layerRuns on scheduleRead only

P4

Amazon stockouts before the next PO

“Which of our top SKUs run out on Amazon before their next purchase order lands?”

What Flieber does
Projects Amazon stock for your top products and compares each stockout date with the arrival of open POs and inbound shipments.
What you get
The products that will run out first, by how many days, and the options to cover the gap.

Inventory forecastingRuns onceRead only

P5

Weekly replenishment

“Every Monday, review the products that may need replenishment and recommend what to order. Consider current stock, forecast demand, on-order inventory, supplier lead time, MOQ and target coverage. Group the recommendations by supplier and explain the reasoning.”

What Flieber does
Runs replenishment for every product and location with your planning parameters and order constraints applied.
What you get
Recommended orders grouped by supplier, each with the reasoning behind the quantity.

Draft POs and send them to your ERP

“Every Monday, draft POs by supplier and send them to NetSuite for approval.”

What Flieber does
Drafts purchase orders by supplier and date, with costs, and once you approve, pushes them into your ERP.
What you get
Draft POs ready for review every Monday; nothing reaches the ERP until you approve.

Six months of buying within budget

“Plan the next six months of orders from this supplier within our budget.”

What Flieber does

What you get
A month-by-month purchase plan for that supplier.

What Flieber does
Simulates the longer lead time and recalculates inventory, stockouts, revenue, margin and cash.

The products that would run out, the sales at stake and what to order earlier.

“Email this PO to the supplier once I approve it.”

What you get
A weekly list of forecasts to review, with the reason each one was flagged.
