02 · Inventory intelligence

Raw transactions in. Decisions you can trust out

Business context turns records into a model of how your business works: which listings are the same product, which components make a bundle, which supplier ships to which warehouse and what a stockout did to last month’s sales. The planning engine then does the math with proprietary algorithms, not language models.

Questions builders ask

The usual ones, answered plainly

Our AI can write this. Why use Flieber?

AI is very good at writing interfaces and glue code. It is much less reliable at inventory logic, such as stockout-corrected demand, bundles, open-PO timing and lead times, and it won’t keep your integrations running. Flieber gives your builders those parts already working, so the code you write sits on something reliable.

Can I buy only the data layer?

Yes. Teams building their own tools with AI can use Flieber’s data layer on its own through MCP and API, and add the other modules any time.

Do I have to use Flieber’s connectors?

No. If your data is already usable, bring it in through CSV files or Google Sheets, or import your own forecasts. Direct connection to your own data warehouse: [CONFIRM BRING-YOUR-OWN-WAREHOUSE SUPPORT]

Can my own AI agent use Flieber?

Yes. Claude, Cursor and any MCP-compatible agent connect through Flieber’s MCP server; your systems can also use the public API.

Will Flieber change anything without my approval?

Not by default. Every change waits for your approval until you set a workflow to run on its own.

Am I locked in?

The layers are selected independently and your data is never sold or shared. Data access and portability are covered in technical discovery. [CONFIRM EXPORT AND PORTABILITY TERMS]
