You can, and most teams start that way. Writing the dashboard is the easy part. Building in-house covers the use cases you face today. Flieber already covers most of the ones you’ll face next.

Questions

The usual ones, answered plainly

Can my own AI agent use Flieber?

Yes. Claude, Cursor and any MCP-compatible agent connect through Flieber’s MCP server; your systems can also use the public API.

Which plan do builders use?

Flieber Self-Serve. It includes the Flieber app plus Flieber’s data, context and inventory logic through MCP and API, priced to your operation. Flieber shows your price as soon as onboarding is done, before you pay anything.

Will Flieber change anything without my approval?

Not by default. Every change waits for your approval until you set a workflow to run on its own.

Where are the API docs?

The MCP and API documentation is in the Flieber app at app.flieber.com/app/developers. A customer login is required.

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. Flieber does not train on customer data, and its AI providers are set up not to. Your data is never sold or shared.
