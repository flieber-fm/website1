Common operating problems

Purchases, promotions, ads, financing: every one is ultimately an inventory decision

Stockouts, excess inventory, spreadsheets at their limit, forecasts nobody trusts. For most product brands inventory is the biggest use of cash, and getting it wrong costs more than almost any other mistake.

Purchases and transfers

A PO locks up cash for months. Buy too much and it sits in a warehouse; buy too little and you stock out.

Promotions

A discount only pays off if you have the units to sell. Run it on thin stock and you trade margin for a stockout.

Ads and influencer campaigns

Demand you pay for is wasted if the product runs out mid-campaign.

Financing

Credit lines and inventory loans are sized on stock value and sell-through. What you can borrow depends on your inventory numbers being right.

Questions planners ask

The usual ones, answered plainly

How much effort is implementation?

Standard setup is included: connect your sales channels and warehouses, configure your key parameters and Flieber starts working. You don’t need engineers. Assisted integrations and simple customizations come with a paid plan.

Will it cover our use case?

Flieber plans finished-goods inventory across channels, warehouses and suppliers, including kits, bundles, backorders, preorders and wholesale. If your process needs something specific, Flieber’s team can tailor it. We’ll tell you plainly what’s standard and what isn’t.

How steep is the learning curve?

The apps follow familiar planning tasks: reviewing forecasts, checking projected stock and approving orders. You can also ask Flieber questions in plain language, in the app or in Slack.

How is it priced?

Flieber Self-Serve is priced on the features you enable and your data volume, and shows your price as soon as onboarding is done, before you pay anything. Managed Services is quoted per brand. Contracts are monthly.

Will it fit our existing process?

Planning parameters, approval rules and reports are configurable, and Flieber connects to your ERP, 3PLs and suppliers. Flieber plans finished-goods inventory and connects to your WMS, ERP or MRP; it doesn’t replace them.
