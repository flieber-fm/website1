What powers every experience

Four layers. Use all of them, or only the ones you need

Every Run application is built on these layers, and builders can select each one on its own. Already have clean data, a forecast or your own interface? Bring it.

  1. Data foundation

    Sales, orders, inventory and shipments arrive from every connected system and are reconciled into one record, with listings mapped to products and bundles converted into components. Optional if your data is already usable.

  2. Inventory intelligence

    The records become a model of how your business works, and a planning engine does the math: forecasts, projections and recommendations computed against your real constraints. Proprietary algorithms, not language models.

  3. Applications

    Ready-to-use apps for demand forecasting, inventory forecasting, replenishment and workflows, or the app you build yourself on the same layers through MCP and API.

  4. Reliability and validation

    Data health checks, sales history corrected for anomalies, forecast accuracy and bias, and decisions that wait for your approval. You can see how well it works before you rely on it.
