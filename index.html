How it works

What your plan runs on

Our planners, your team and every tailored view work from the same foundation, so the team stops assembling data and starts from numbers it can trust.

  1. 1

    Connected data

    Sales, orders, inventory and shipments from your channels, warehouses, 3PLs and suppliers, reconciled into one record and kept current.

    Amazon, Shopify, Walmart, TikTok Shop, eBay, Etsy, BigCommerce and Google Sheets connect in one click. ERPs such as NetSuite, EDI through SPS Commerce, accounting systems and more than 20 3PL and warehouse systems are set up by our team.

  2. 2

    Business context

    Which listings are the same product, which components make a bundle, which supplier ships to which warehouse and what a stockout did to last month’s sales.

  3. 3

    Forecasting and replenishment logic

    Forecasts by product and channel, corrected for stockouts and promotions, and purchase and transfer recommendations within your MOQs, case packs, lead times and cash. Proprietary algorithms, not language models, do the math.

        Questions

        What teams ask us first

        What can Flieber help me do, and what will my team still handle?

        Flieber connects and reconciles your data, forecasts demand, projects inventory and recommends what to buy or move, in views and workflows that fit how you plan. Your team sets the objectives, adds what no system knows and makes every decision that commits cash. If you add ongoing support, our planners help with the analysis and the planning routine; the decisions stay with you.

        Do your planners make decisions for us?

        No. Your team makes the calls; our planners help you make them with the best data and context. If you choose, our team carries out the orders and follow-ups you approve.

        Can I use Flieber without ongoing services?

        Yes. Ongoing planning support (Managed Services) is a separate, optional engagement. Your team can use Flieber as it is, or any system you customize, on its own.

        What can be tailored, and how do we agree on scope?

        Views, reports, dashboards and workflows, built around your process and rules on top of Flieber’s shared data connections and planning logic. Before any work starts, we agree on what will be built, who maintains it and how it’s priced.

        If my own team builds or runs the tools, what do I keep paying Flieber for?

        Ongoing use of the Flieber platform: the data connections, business context and planning logic your tools run on. Customization by our team and Managed Services are charged separately, and only if you choose them.

        How much does it cost?

        It depends on your operation and on the help you choose. We share pricing in the first conversation, once we understand how you plan.

        Does my team need to know AI to use Flieber?

        No. Your team works in views built for how it plans. AI works in the background: it takes repetitive work off our planners and your team, speeds up tailoring and explains results in plain language.

        Can Flieber change anything in our systems on its own?

        Only within the approval rules you set. By default, every change Flieber makes to your systems requires approval.
