The problem

Your data isn’t a set of facts. It’s a moving system.

Every system you sell and buy through sees a slice of the truth. None of them reconcile it. Hand that to an AI as-is and every answer is confidently out of date.

Stockouts hide real demand.

Zero sales on a day you had zero stock isn’t zero demand.

Bundles consume components.

Kits, bundles, preorders and backorders all draw on the same units.

Channels compete for units.

A wholesale order, an Amazon inbound and a DTC promo can all claim the same pallet.

Ads and price changes distort history.

Last month’s spike may have been a promotion, not demand.

What Flieber does with your data

Consolidated. Contextualized. Up to date.

Flieber brings every source together and adds what none of them know, with inventory at the center because it is where every decision meets reality.

Fit

Honest fit. We’d rather you, or your agent, know now.

Built for brands that:

  • Sell on two or more channels: Amazon, Walmart, DTC, wholesale or retail
  • Manage catalog complexity: multiple SKUs per product, kits and bundles, or backorders
  • Run a complex operation: multiple warehouses, overseas suppliers, long lead times or high MOQs
  • Already work in Claude or Slack, or are building their own agents

Probably not for you if:

  • You sell on one channel and a spreadsheet still works
  • You’re looking for a warehouse management system or a full ERP
  • You plan manufacturing runs (MRP) rather than buying finished goods
