Why inventory

Purchases, promotions, ads, financing: every one is ultimately an inventory decision.

For most product brands, inventory is the biggest use of cash. Every major decision either adds to it, draws it down, or depends on it being there. That’s why Flieber starts with inventory, and why getting it wrong costs more than almost any other mistake.

Purchases and transfers.

A PO locks up cash for months. Buy too much and it sits in a warehouse; buy too little and you stock out.

Promotions.

A discount only pays off if you have the units to sell. Run it on thin stock and you trade margin for a stockout.

Ads and influencer campaigns.

Demand you pay for is wasted if the product runs out mid-campaign.

Financing.

Credit lines and inventory loans are sized on stock value and sell-through. What you can borrow depends on your inventory numbers being right.

Know the position.
