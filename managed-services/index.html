What you get

What Managed Services is

Managed Services pairs the Flieber tool, tailored to your brand, with a team of Flieber specialists who work where planning, operations and technology meet. The result: better decisions, streamlined execution and automations that make your team’s work much easier.

Every plan includes

A Flieber tool tailored to you

Your requests, built in

Continuous improvement

A team of specialists

Plans

Three plans

Choose the plan that fits your operation today, and move up or down as your needs change.

Lite

A second set of eyes on all your data and planning

You plan, decide and run operations. Our specialists watch everything so nothing slips and you get the full value of Flieber.

  • Keep Flieber updated Data, SKU mappings, new products set up, stores, lead times, MOQs and settings, always current.

  • Integration issues solved Not only flagged: we follow each one until it’s fixed.

  • Early flags What needs your attention: unmapped SKUs, products to set up, data that looks wrong and decisions only you can make.

  • Forecast drift Once forecasts are approved, we review drift closely.

  • Your context in Flieber We gather context from your team and documents and add it to Flieber.

  • Regular reviews Every two weeks with your team, plus a monthly management meeting.

Core

Planning, together

Everything in Lite, plus our specialists plan alongside your team:

  • A weekly working session Time every week to plan together.

  • Forecast reviews With full, thorough reports.

  • Purchase and replenishment plans Built with you, and approved plans turned into purchase-order files.

  • Your S&OP Our specialists take part in it.

  • Your call on every plan You approve every plan and order, send the orders and run operations.

Full

Planning and operations

Everything in Core, plus operational execution:

  • Purchase orders and production We place POs and follow up with suppliers until production is done.

  • Freight Forwarder quotes and bookings, and following shipments and customs with your forwarder and broker.

  • Logistics to the warehouse Following logistics to the warehouse and reconciling received quantities.

  • Workflow automations Automations for execution.

  • Your call, your terms You still approve every plan and order and keep supplier terms, payments and budget.

Questions

Questions

Do your specialists make decisions for us?

No. Your team makes every decision; our specialists work alongside it with the best data and context. On Full, our team carries out the orders and follow-ups you approve.

What’s the difference between Lite, Core and Full?

Lite is a second set of eyes on all your data and planning: our specialists watch everything so nothing slips. Core adds planning together: a weekly working session, forecast reviews, purchase and replenishment plans and your S&OP. Full adds operational execution after your approval: purchase orders, supplier follow-up, freight, logistics to the warehouse and workflow automations.

Which plan is right for us?

Lite if your team runs planning and operations and wants a second set of eyes on everything. Core if you want to plan together with our specialists. Full if you also want support with operations, from purchase orders to the warehouse. Talk to a specialist about what fits your operation.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. You can move up or down as your needs change.

Who are Flieber’s specialists?

Planners, data specialists, operators and engineers who master the Flieber platform and draw on experience from more than 1,000 brands since 2019.

Who do we work with day to day?

Dedicated account people, backed by a team of specialists: planners, data specialists, operators and engineers.

What is the Flieber tool tailored to us?

A complete Flieber tool, with demand forecast, inventory forecast and replenishment planning, in its own space on Flieber’s servers, at its own address (yourbrand.flieber.com), with your logo and colours, tuned to your needs, use cases and preferences. Every plan includes it.

What hours and time zones do your specialists work in?

As a rule, 8am to 6pm Eastern Time. We can adjust for customers in very different time zones, such as Europe and Australia.

Which languages do your specialists speak?

English is the team’s main language, and the team includes native speakers of other languages, such as Spanish and Portuguese.

What access do your specialists have to our data and systems?

The access you choose to give them. At a minimum, they have full access to the data in your Flieber account. The more context you share from outside Flieber, the better their work.

Can we use our own AI agents with Managed Services?

Yes. Your agents connect to Flieber through MCP or API and work from the same data and context as your team and our specialists.

Our data lives partly in spreadsheets. Is that a problem?

No. Flieber connects natively to most systems. Where you still rely on spreadsheets or disconnected systems, our specialists watch that data, flag what looks wrong and follow integration issues until they’re fixed.

How do promotions and launches get into the forecast?

Our specialists gather context from your team and documents, such as promotions, launches and channel changes, and add it to Flieber.

Do your specialists talk to our suppliers and freight forwarders?

On Full, yes: supplier follow-up until production is done, forwarder quotes and bookings, and following shipments and customs with your forwarder and broker. On Lite and Core, your team keeps those relationships.

Is Managed Services custom software development?

No. Requests that make your Flieber tool work better for your team are built into it as part of the service.

Is there a free trial of Managed Services?

No. Managed Services is tailored to your operation from day one and takes real work from our team. You can try the platform itself with a 14-day Self-Serve trial on your own data.

Can we start with Self-Serve and add Managed Services later?

Yes, at any time.
