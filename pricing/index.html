Questions

The usual ones, answered plainly.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Start free for 14 days, no credit card required. Connect your data and see Flieber work on your operation before you pay anything.

How does pricing work?

Flieber Self-Serve starts at $149/month. The price scales with the features you enable and your data volume, using sales, number of channels and number of warehouses as the proxy. Flieber Managed Services is quoted per brand, after a conversation with a planner.

Is there an annual commitment?

No. Contracts are monthly.

What does setup look like?

Standard setup is included: connect your sales channels and warehouses, configure your key parameters and Flieber starts working. Customizations come with a paid plan.

Can I switch between Self-Serve and Managed Services?

Yes. Both run on the same platform, so you can switch or combine them any time.

Do I need to replace my ERP or IMS?

No. Flieber sits on top of what you already use. If you don’t have an ERP or IMS, Flieber can connect directly to the data source you prefer.

What if my data lives in spreadsheets?

That works too. Flieber’s Google Sheets integration reads the data you keep in your spreadsheets, so you don’t maintain the same information in two places.
