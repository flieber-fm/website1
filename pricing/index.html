Questions

The usual ones, answered plainly

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Start free for 14 days, no credit card required. Connect your data through our native integrations and see Flieber work on your operation before you pay anything. Assisted integrations are set up once you’re on a paid plan.

How does pricing work?

Flieber Self-Serve is priced on the features you enable and your data volume. Flieber Managed Services is quoted per brand, after a conversation with a planner. Book a demo and we’ll share the exact price for your brand.

Is there an annual commitment?

No. Contracts are monthly.

What does setup look like?

Standard setup is included: connect your sales channels and warehouses, configure your key parameters and Flieber starts working. Assisted integrations and customizations come with a paid plan.

Can I switch between Self-Serve and Managed Services?

Yes. Both run on the same platform, so you can switch or combine them any time.

Do I need to replace my ERP or IMS?

No. Flieber sits on top of what you already use. If you don’t have an ERP or IMS, Flieber can connect directly to the data source you prefer.

What if my data lives in spreadsheets?

That works too. Flieber’s Google Sheets integration reads the data you keep in your spreadsheets, so you don’t maintain the same information in two places.
