The problem

Why channels drift apart

01

Channels behave differently

A marketplace sells every hour, a wholesale account orders in large, irregular batches, and each has its own lead times and replenishment path.

02

Separate plans over-order

When each channel is planned on its own, every plan keeps its own safety stock and the business carries more inventory than it needs.

03

Data lives in too many places

Orders, stock and shipments sit in marketplaces, 3PLs, ERPs and spreadsheets, and someone has to stitch them together by hand.
