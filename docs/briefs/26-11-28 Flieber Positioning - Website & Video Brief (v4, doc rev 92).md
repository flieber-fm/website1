# Flieber Positioning: Website & Video Brief
2026-11-27 · @Fabricio Miranda

## How to use this brief
This is the single source for Flieber's positioning on the website and in videos. It replaces the October messaging. Where this brief and older material disagree, this brief wins.

Website agent: use sections 1 to 8. Section 8 is the homepage structure; sections 1 to 7 are the rules every page follows.

Video agent: use sections 1 to 7 and section 9. Videos carry only the stable story: the shift to a new way of planning, how Flieber gets the team there, and the moments brands bring us in.

Settled: everything in sections 1 to 9. Not settled: section 10. Do not fill those gaps with your own claims.
No em dashes in any copy.

## 1. Positioning
Tagline: "Flieber: combining AI and specialists to power your inventory decisions."
Short form: "Flieber: where AI meets inventory experts."
Use the tagline wherever there is room to explain: the homepage, decks, email signatures, the site's meta description. Use the short form where space is tight and context does the rest: the logo lockup, video end cards, social bios, banners. Write both exactly as above, with a lowercase word after the colon.
Full statement:
AI is changing how commerce brands plan. Routine work will run on its own, any context a brand has, from past decisions to cash plans, will feed every plan, and intelligence will build up instead of living in a few people's heads. Inventory sits at the center of it, because purchasing, pricing, advertising and cash flow all depend on it.
Most brands know this shift is coming. Few know how to get from the way they work today to the new way, and building it alone usually stalls. Flieber gets them there: a platform tailored to each brand, combining AI with specialists who've seen how more than 1,000 brands operate. The team keeps every call, and the system gets smarter with every cycle.
What changed from October: the team is no longer a separate service next to the software. The team is how the system gets built around each brand and how it keeps getting better. We lead with the outcome and the people, and the tool is the means.

Never use "Managed Services" on the website or in videos. It sounds like outsourcing and triggers "I already have a team."

The 2020 test: before any line goes live, ask whether it describes a better version of the old way (faster spreadsheets, nicer dashboards, more accurate reports) or a new way of operating (work that runs itself, data the team never had, intelligence that builds up). Only the second belongs on the site.
"Humans" in the H1, "specialists" everywhere else: the H1 says "humans" on purpose, for the contrast with "AI." Body copy and the tagline say "specialists," and the short form says "experts." Keep each word where it is; do not change one into the other.
Tone: plain, confident, specific. Write for a busy executive: short sentences, no jargon, no hype words such as "revolutionary" or "game-changing." Never name competitors.

The homepage tells one story from top to bottom: the shift to a new way of planning, what that new way looks like, how Flieber gets your team there, why it works, and the next step. Replace the current homepage and hero now. Keep the Self-Serve and data-layer pages, moved off the main path.

The new era of planning

Automations that get smarter with every cycle. Routine work runs on its own and improves each time it runs.

Data and context without limits. Not just sales, inventory and purchase orders. Add marketing plans, cash flow, competitive information, and the history of past decisions and why you made them. Flieber's AI takes all of it into account in every forecast and every plan. And it builds with every use: each new piece of context is kept and applied from then on, so the system keeps learning from it.

Intelligence that compounds. Every exception resolved and every outcome observed feeds back in, so insights build on each other, alerts get sharper and the automations keep improving.

Closing line: "All in a platform tailored to your brand, with specialists who've seen how more than 1,000 brands operate. Welcome to the new era of planning."

The gap. Three short points: everyone knows planning is changing; few teams know how to get from today's way of working to the new one; building it alone is slow and usually stalls.

How Flieber gets you there, in three steps:

We learn how you operate: we connect your data, join your planning meetings and capture the context behind your numbers. Setup takes about the first month, and you see value before it ends.

We tailor your platform: your channels, suppliers, rules and views.

We stay and it keeps getting smarter: your specialists stay with the account, the routine moves onto automations cycle after cycle, your team approves every plan, and every month you get a scorecard on the measures we agree on together.

When brands bring us in (section 5): the six moments as short cards, each with the prospect's words and our answer.

Why not build it yourself? "Brands that partner on AI succeed about three times as often as those that build it alone, according to MIT's 2025 study of enterprise AI. We bring the platform, the specialists and what we've learned from more than 1,000 brands, so you don't start from zero." Cite the source on the page (section 6).

Beyond inventory: the one line from section 4.

Proof strip (section 6): 1,000+ brands since 2019, 100+ brands today, the Nixtla result, integration logos. Customer logos only once approved.

Flieber Self-Serve, one short block: "Want to run Flieber yourself, or build your own tools on our data? Use the app, CLI and API directly." Link to the Self-Serve page.

FAQ

"I already have a great team." Good: we work with them, not instead of them. The routine moves onto automations, new data reaches them every day, and they spend their time on the calls that matter.

"Why not build this ourselves with AI?" You can, but most in-house AI projects stall. Partnering with specialists succeeds about three times as often, and you get what we've learned from more than 1,000 brands from day one.

"Do I lose control?" No. Your team approves every plan and every order.

"Do your specialists leave after setup?" No. They stay with your account, and your platform keeps getting smarter with every cycle.

"How do I know it's working?" Every month you get a scorecard on the measures we agree on together.

"Why does it cost more than software?" Because it is more than software: a platform tailored to your brand, automations and data your team could not build alone, and specialists who work with your team. Inventory shapes your most important decisions, and one avoided stockout or overstock on a bestseller can pay for a year.

"What happens in the first month?" We connect your data, set up your platform and learn how you operate. You see value before the month ends.

"Can I just use the tool?" Yes. Flieber Self-Serve gives you the app, CLI and API.

Final call to action: "See what your operation looks like in the new era of planning." Button: "Talk to a specialist."
