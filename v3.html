Questions

How is this different from the AI tools my marketplaces offer?

Each of those sees one channel. Flieber joins all of them with your warehouses, 3PLs, ERP and suppliers, so the answer counts every place a product is sold and stored.

Can my agent change anything without me?

No. Every write is a preview first and waits for your confirmation. Approval is required by default, and you decide whether anything runs on its own.

How fresh is the data?

Every answer shows how old its data is. If a store is disconnected, Flieber says so and gives you the link to reconnect it.

Do I have to replace my ERP or WMS?

No. Flieber reads from them and sends approved decisions back. They stay your system of record.

Do you train on my data?

No. Your data is never sold or shared, never used for training, and the AI providers Flieber uses are set up not to train on it either.

Which AI assistants work with Flieber?

Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and any agent that supports MCP. You can also use the Flieber app, Slack or the API.

How is it priced?

Priced to your operation: your stores, data volume and the features you use. Your price is shown after onboarding, with unlimited users on every plan.
