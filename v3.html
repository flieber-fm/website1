What Flieber is

Three layers behind every answer

Your AI is the front door. Flieber is the source of truth and judgment it calls.

01 · Data layer

Reconciled truth

Every channel, warehouse, 3PL, ERP and supplier, connected, mapped and corrected into one model of your business.

  • Listings mapped to products across channels
  • Inventory by location, inbound and in transit
  • Corrected history next to raw history
  • Data age and disconnected stores shown on every answer
02 · Planning engine

Math your AI can't do

Forecasts, projections, recommendations and simulations, computed by purpose-built models, not by a language model.

  • Many models, the best one chosen per listing
  • Accuracy measured and shown
  • MOQs, case packs, containers, lead times and cash built in
  • Same inputs, same answer, with the reasoning attached
03 · Decision tools

Room for the hard calls

When a decision needs scenarios, scale or a team, your AI opens a tool built and refined with operators.

  • A card inside the conversation
  • A full dashboard when the decision needs room
  • Preview and approval before anything is written
  • Every approved decision kept on the plan of record

How it works

From a question to a decision on record

Simple questions get answers. Decisions that commit cash or stock get a tool, a preview and your approval.

Ask in plain language

Your AI calls Flieber's tools and relays a short, plain answer with the evidence behind it, including how fresh the data is.

# in your AI assistant
you > Which products will stock out before the next container lands?
ai  > calling flieber.inventory_risk ...
ai  > A short list, ranked by lost sales. Sources: all stores connected.
ai  > Want me to open the purchase planner for these?

Change an input, watch the plan move

Scenario analysis across a whole catalog is a computation problem. The tool runs the engine, so the answer comes back fast and the same view is there next week.

Built to be trusted with cash

Agents can call it. Only you approve it

01Preview before every write

Every change arrives as a preview card and waits for an explicit confirmation. Approval is required by default.

02Edit causes, not numbers

Tell Flieber why demand will change. The reason is stored as intent, survives recalculation and is flagged if the base moves.

03Evidence on every answer

Recommendations show their reasoning, the data they used and its age. A disconnected store is never hidden in a summary.

04Measured, not assumed

Forecast accuracy is tracked and shown against a simple benchmark, so you can see what the engine adds.

05The same answer twice

Same inputs, same result. Two people asking the same question see the same view.

06Connects, never replaces

Your ERP, WMS and 3PL stay the system of record for what they do. Flieber reads from them and sends approved decisions back.

Your data is never sold or shared No training on your data AI providers set up not to train on it either
