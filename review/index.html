Open decisions

What only you can settle

  • Free trial. Left out, as in Option 6. Every path goes through “Talk to us”. If the trial stays, it comes back as a second action for the team that plans on its own.
  • Does AI really make the service affordable? The “Why now” line rests on it. If planner hours per brand don’t come down, this lead turns Flieber into a planning agency.
  • Tailoring inside Managed Services or separate? Option 2 says custom builds are a separate project. The site keeps them priced separately, but the positioning treats them as one service.
  • Evidence. The 1,000+ brands wording and the +38 / -62 / -17 / -88 results still need the check the Oct 14 brief asks for.
New wording

Lines written for Option 7

Everything else reuses approved claims from Options 2, 4 and 6. These lines are new and need your approval.

  • “Your inventory planning, done with you” and the hero subhead
  • “Our planners join your team. Your team keeps the decisions”
  • “Planning help like this used to be hard to justify” and the “AI changes that” line
  • “Plan with Flieber’s planners” and “Plan with your own team” (tree step 1)
  • “Flieber’s standard planning views, reports and workflows”, which replaces Option 6’s unconfirmed “ready-made templates”
  • The /agents summary paragraph and “Has planners with time to plan”
