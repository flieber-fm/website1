What it does for you

Three jobs, done well.

Get recommended quantities

Order quantities that account for demand, current stock, inbound supply, MOQs and safety stock.

Order at the right time

Order-by dates based on actual supplier lead times, not just quoted ones.

Review with the reasoning visible

Each recommendation shows why, so planners can approve quickly or override with confidence.

Inputs & outputs

What goes in, and what you get out.

Inputs and integrations

  • Inventory state and open purchase orders (required)
  • Demand assumptions: Flieber forecast or your own (required)
  • Supply parameters: lead times, MOQs, case packs (required)
  • Budget or capacity constraints (optional)

Outputs and decisions supported

  • Recommended order quantities by SKU and supplier
  • Order-by dates
  • Reasoning behind each recommendation
  • Draft purchase orders for review

Performance & accuracy

Decisions you can check against outcomes.

Flieber compares recommendations with what was ordered and with the stock that followed, so you can see whether the logic is working.

How it’s measured
  • Recommended vs. ordered vs. realized outcomes
  • Lead times measured from actual receipts
  • Visibility into overrides and their results
  • Alerts when supply parameters look wrong

Fit & flexibility

Works with what you have. Adapts to how you work.

Dependencies

Needs inventory state, demand assumptions and supply parameters. Works best with Inventory planning and Demand forecasting.

We’ll confirm the right combination for your business during your demo.

Customization options

Flieber’s team can tailor this application for you, with no code from your side.

  • Your own safety stock or coverage rules
  • Approval thresholds by value, supplier or category
  • Container, pallet or case-pack optimization
  • Purchase orders pushed to your ERP or supplier portal

Questions

Frequently asked

Will it place orders automatically?

Recommendations are reviewed and approved by your team. Where it makes sense, approved orders can be handed off to connected systems. Ask us about your setup.

Can it handle MOQs and case packs?

Yes. Supply parameters such as minimum order quantities and case packs are part of the recommendation logic.

What if we already have a forecast?

You can use it. Replenishment needs demand assumptions, which can come from Flieber or from you.
