Questions planners ask

Frequently asked

How much effort is implementation?

Most of the work is connecting your data and agreeing on planning settings, and our team guides both. You don’t need engineers. The timeline depends on your channels and data, and we’ll give you a realistic estimate during your demo.

Will it cover our use case?

The applications cover forecasting, inventory projection, replenishment and operational workflows. If your process needs something specific, Flieber can customize the application. We’ll tell you plainly what’s standard and what would need customization.

How steep is the learning curve?

The apps are built around familiar planning tasks: reviewing forecasts, checking projected stock, approving orders. A Flieber planner works with your team during setup.

How is it priced?

Pricing depends on the applications you use and the scale of your business. We’ll walk you through options during a demo.

Will it fit our existing process?

Lead times, safety stock, review cycles, approvals and layouts can be configured, and anything beyond that can be tailored by our team. Flieber should follow your process, not replace it.
