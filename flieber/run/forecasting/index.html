What it does for you

Three jobs, done well.

Forecast demand at the level you plan

By SKU, channel and location, over the horizons that match your lead times.

Know how much to trust the forecast

Forecast vs. actual, error and bias tracked over time, so you can see where the forecast is reliable and where it isn’t.

Add what only your team knows

Record adjustments for promotions, launches and events, and see whether they helped.

Inputs & outputs

What goes in, and what you get out.

Inputs and integrations

  • Historical sales by SKU and channel (required)
  • Stockout history, so lost sales don’t bias demand
  • Planned promotions and launches (optional)
  • Product relationships such as SKU replacements (optional)

Outputs and decisions supported

  • Demand forecast by SKU, channel and horizon
  • Forecast accuracy and bias trends
  • Planner adjustments with before/after comparison
  • Demand input for inventory planning and replenishment

Performance & accuracy

Accuracy you can see, not just a claim.

Every forecast is measured against what actually sold. You see accuracy by SKU, channel and horizon, and where it falls short.

How it’s measured
  • Historical forecast vs. actual, period by period
  • Error and bias trends over time
  • Impact of planner adjustments
  • Stockout periods excluded so demand isn’t underestimated

Fit & flexibility

Works with what you have. Adapts to how you work.

Dependencies

Needs relevant historical demand data. Feeds Inventory planning and Replenishment, which can also use a forecast you already have.

We’ll confirm the right combination for your business during your demo.

Customization options

Flieber’s team can tailor this application for you, with no code from your side.

  • Forecast grouping by your own categories or hierarchies
  • Custom horizons and review cadences
  • Approval steps for large overrides
  • Additional demand signals specific to your business

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need clean historical data?

You need relevant sales history. Flieber’s data foundation handles consolidation and mapping across channels, and we’ll check data readiness with you during setup.

Can I adjust the forecast?

Yes. Planners can record adjustments for promotions, launches or other known events, and Flieber tracks whether each adjustment improved accuracy.

How accurate will it be?

It depends on your products and data, and no forecast is perfect. Instead of quoting a number up front, Flieber measures accuracy continuously and shows it to you.
