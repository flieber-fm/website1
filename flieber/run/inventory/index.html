What it does for you

Three jobs, done well.

See projected inventory

Week by week, by SKU and location, including open purchase orders and transfers.

Catch stockouts early

Know which SKUs will run out, where, and when, while there’s still time to act.

Find excess before it ages

Spot overstock exposure so you can slow orders, rebalance or promote.

Inputs & outputs

What goes in, and what you get out.

Inputs and integrations

  • Current inventory positions by location (required)
  • Demand forecast from Flieber, or your own forecast
  • Open purchase orders and expected arrival dates
  • Transfers between locations (optional)

Outputs and decisions supported

  • Projected inventory by SKU, location and week
  • Stockout and overstock exposure with timing
  • Weeks of cover by SKU and location
  • Input for replenishment recommendations

Performance & accuracy

Projections you can trace and verify.

Every projected number can be traced back to its inputs: on hand, forecast demand, inbound supply. Projection error is measured against what was actually on hand.

How it’s measured
  • Trace any projection to its source inputs
  • Projected vs. actual inventory over time
  • Alerts when inputs are stale or incomplete
  • Inbound ETAs updated as they change

Questions

Frequently asked

Can I use my own forecast?

Yes. Inventory planning can use an imported demand forecast instead of Flieber’s, as long as it meets the minimum input format.

Does it handle multiple warehouses and channels?

Projections are calculated by SKU and location, so you can see exposure wherever inventory is held. We’ll confirm your specific locations during setup.

What happens when a PO is late?

When an expected arrival date changes, the projection updates, so you can see the effect on stock coverage.
