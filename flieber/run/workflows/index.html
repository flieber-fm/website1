What it does for you

Three jobs, done well.

Route decisions to the right people

Approvals by value, supplier or category, so the right person signs off on each order.

Get alerted when things change

Stockout risk, late shipments and large forecast changes are flagged to the right owner.

Hand off without re-keying

Approved decisions move to connected systems and teams without copying data by hand.

Inputs & outputs

What goes in, and what you get out.

Inputs and integrations

  • Outputs from other Flieber applications
  • Your approval rules and owners
  • Connected systems for handoffs (tool-specific)
  • Notification channels your team uses

Outputs and decisions supported

  • Approval queues with clear owners
  • Alerts and operational triggers
  • Audit trail of reviews and decisions
  • Handoffs to connected systems

Performance & accuracy

A clear record of every decision.

Every review, override and approval is recorded, so you can see what was decided, by whom, and what happened next.

How it’s measured
  • Full audit trail of approvals and overrides
  • Decision vs. outcome comparisons
  • Alerts on stale or incomplete inputs
  • Handoff status across teams

Questions

Frequently asked

What workflows are included?

Common planning workflows such as reviews, approvals, alerts and handoffs. Specialized tools depend on your use case, and we’ll go through what’s available during a demo.

Can Flieber build a workflow specific to us?

Yes. Customizing workflows is one of the most common requests, and our team can tailor them without your team writing code.

Which systems can it connect to?

It depends on the workflow and your stack. We’ll confirm supported connections for your setup before you commit.
