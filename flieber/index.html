Made for the way you operate

Fit the software to your process, not the other way around.

Your suppliers, channels and approval rules are specific to your business. Flieber is built so the software can follow them.

  • Fields: track the attributes your team actually plans with.
  • Workflows: add reviews, approvals and handoffs where they belong.
  • Layouts: group and filter by supplier, channel, category or region.
  • Calculations: set safety stock, lead time and coverage rules to match how you operate.

What you can adjust depends on the application. We’ll show you exactly what’s configurable for your use case.
