Why we’re changing

One-size-fits-all no longer fits.

For years, inventory planning software has meant one choice: adopt a vendor’s product and adapt your process to it, or build your own and rebuild the hard parts from scratch.

AI-assisted development changes that. Building custom software is getting easier, but correct inventory logic, reliable data and trustworthy validation are still hard to get right.

So Flieber now offers both: proven applications you can use today, and the foundation behind them for teams who want to build their own.

What we believe

Principles we build by.

Value today, a better fit tomorrow

Customers shouldn’t have to choose between getting value now and owning a better-fitting solution later. Start ready, then make it yours.

Modular by design

Every layer can stand on its own. Bring your own data, your own forecast or your own interface, and use only what you need.

Measure, don’t promise

We don’t claim perfect accuracy or zero maintenance. We measure forecast and decision accuracy and show you the results.

Building isn’t required

Building your own isn’t better for every company. Many teams are best served by a great application, tailored by us when needed.
